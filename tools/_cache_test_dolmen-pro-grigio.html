Large stone effect porcelain tiles

Stone effect porcelain tiles: effects and characteristics

Stone effect porcelain tiles deliver the full experience for the senses with warm realism, sculptural textures and a nuanced colour palette. The ideal surface solution for domestic and public spaces, these high-end materials will deliver dimension and added textural interest that mimics the character and one-of-a-kind feeling of stone.

The wide variety of tones - from light to dark grey, brown, beige and white - makes natural stone look porcelain tiles suitable for public and private spaces and ideal for use as porcelain tiles for floors and walls, porcelain tiles for exterior walls, porcelain table tops, porcelain kitchen worktops, kitchen islands and bathroom furniture.

To satisfy various project requirements, large stone look ceramic tiles can come in 6mm, 12mm and 20mm thicknesses in rectified and not rectified large format sizes.

The robust composition of stone look porcelain tiles makes this material resistant to impact and abrasion, durable and waterproof, allowing it to be used both indoors and as stone effect porcelain outdoor tiles for ventilated façades and outdoor worktops. Discover natural stone effect floor tiles, stone effect porcelain wall tiles and stone effect porcelain kitchen floor tiles for every room in the house.

Large stone effect porcelain styles: how to match them

Atlas Plan large stone effect floor tiles complement a diverse range of styles, from the most traditional to the most sophisticated.

Warm-toned porcelain limestone look tiles such as Boost Stone Ivory match with equally warm-toned timbers. Pair its minimal spirit with concrete effect floor tiles that celebrate the purity of natural materials. Large format porcelain tiles impress as kitchen countertops and sinks and match elegantly with timber cabinetry.

Our Boost Smoke grey porcelain slabs represent the ultimate in design versatility. Choose grey stone effect floor tiles indoors and match them with stone and concrete styles.

For a tactile, high-end space, marble and stone are a perfect pairing. To accomplish a sleek, sophisticated look, tiles in a similar colour palette are a safe match.

Desert Soul beige marble-effect porcelain furniture coverings balance effortlessly with beige-hued stone effect porcelain floor tiles. Or create a contemporary colour contrast with our Basaltina Volcano grey stone-effect slabs on countertops and sinks and clad walls with light-toned large marble-effect tiles such as Travertino Sand.
